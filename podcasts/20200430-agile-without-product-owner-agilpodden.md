## 77. Agile Without Product Owner [Eng]

We have Maaret Pyhärjärvi with us that has tested to removed their product owner in their development team. She has also removed scrum, jira, estimates and projects and have because of that also improved their performance. We talk about why they have done this and what results they have seen!
